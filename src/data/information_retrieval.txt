Information Retrieval

Information retrieval is the process of finding useful information in a
collection of documents. A search engine usually normalizes text, splits it
into terms, and builds an index that maps terms to documents.
When a user submits a query, the engine looks up matching terms and ranks the
results. Ranking can consider term frequency, document frequency, and how
closely the query terms occur together.
Good search results should be relevant, fast to retrieve, and easy to inspect.
